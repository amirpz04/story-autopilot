The system downloads the file and converts it, which takes about a minute. It appears here after the page refreshes (up to ~12 minutes).

Where to find music you're allowed to use

Only add music you have the right to use. Safe places: Wikimedia Commons audio (open a file, copy the "Original file" link), incompetech.com (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY: credit needed), Free Music Archive (check each song's license) and Pixabay Music (no credit needed, but its site blocks automatic downloads, so download the file yourself and host it somewhere with a direct link). If the license says "CC BY", paste the credit line it asks for. If it says "NonCommercial" or "No Derivatives", don't use it. Even free songs are sometimes flagged by Instagram's copyright system; if a Reel gets muted, remove that song.

How many posts and Reels per day

Same stories: no extra research, but a follower can see a story once as a post and once as a Reel (they go out about 6 or more hours apart). Own stories: nobody sees a story twice, but the research writes posts + Reels stories a day, which costs more Claude usage and thins the quality.

Takes effect from the next daily research run. Posts and Reels never go out within 45 minutes of each other.

Spare posts

Spares are finished posts the daily research run prepares in advance. If you don't like a scheduled post, open it and press Replace with a spare. Each spare uses more of your Claude plan, so 1 is a good start.
